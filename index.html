How to enter mathematical answers

Use plain expressions: 1/3, sqrt(2), pi, i, exp(-t), sin(2*t), or e^(-t). Use parentheses around an entire exponent. Use conj(z) for the complex conjugate of z. ln and log both mean the natural logarithm. Enter only the expression, without u(t)=.

Equivalent expressions are accepted. Roots may be entered in any order. For a matrix, fill in each entry separately. For singularities, use names such as simple pole, pole of order 2, or removable singularity. Each question is worth 4 points; all its fields must be correct for those points. An unrecognised expression is marked “check notation”; you can edit it and check again.
